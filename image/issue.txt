============================================================
 SBX SANDBOX VM - READ BEFORE USE
============================================================
 A VM limits damage. It is NOT a hardware security boundary.
 A VM escape lands on the host. Do not treat this as safe.

 The strongest setup puts the SANDBOX on its own device that
 knows only this core's address. The core is its gateway and
 decides what the sandbox's traffic may reach. Set it up
 exactly that way (see SETUP.md, "Full mode").

 Lite mode (this VM, one kernel shared by core and sandbox)
 is the convenient option, not the locked-down one.

 Keep the firewall in default-deny, and also enforce egress
 at the hypervisor/host, or a compromised guest can change it.

 Token:    sudo cat /etc/sbxfw/token
