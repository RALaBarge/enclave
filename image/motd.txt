============================================================
 SBX SANDBOX VM - READ BEFORE USE
============================================================
 A VM limits damage. It is NOT a hardware security boundary.
 A VM escape lands on the host. Do not treat this as safe.

 For real security: run this on a disposable machine with no
 route to anything you care about, and keep the firewall in
 default-deny.

 Lite mode shares one kernel between harness and sandbox.
 Full mode puts the sandbox on a separate physical box.

 The egress firewall in this VM must ALSO be enforced at the
 hypervisor/host, or a compromised guest can change it.

 Token:    sudo cat /etc/sbxfw/token
