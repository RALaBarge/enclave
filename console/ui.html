Lite mode is the convenient option, not the locked-down one. This VM limits the damage a misbehaving agent or tool can do, but a VM escape lands on the host. The strongest setup puts the sandbox on its own device that knows only the core's address; the core is its gateway and decides what the sandbox's traffic may reach (see SETUP.md, Full mode). Keep the firewall below in default-deny. Nothing here protects data you give the agent.
